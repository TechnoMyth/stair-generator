# Stair Generator

Procedural staircases and railings for game engines, at real-world sizes. Set a staircase's rise, layout and style and
it is generated on the spot: straight, landing, L and U stairs, winders, curved and spiral stairs, with nosed treads,
closed or cut stringers, turned balusters, newel posts, moulded handrails, curtails and volutes, plus railings for
galleries and stairwells. Stairs come with collision that's comfortable to walk and teleport on, levels of detail, and
baking to meshes for shipping.

## Choose your engine

- **[Unity](unity/index.md)**: the Stair Generator package for Unity 6.3 and later.
- **Unreal Engine**: in development.
